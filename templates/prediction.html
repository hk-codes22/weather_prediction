Estimating vs. forecasting

Estimating (what this page does): you give today's temperature, humidity, pressure and wind speed, and the model estimates how much rain goes with those conditions.

Forecasting (not done here): using the recent history of many days to predict rain on future days. That needs a time-series model and is listed under future improvements.
